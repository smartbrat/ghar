How a brand & its people get added and promoted on Ghar.tv

When a business joins, two pages are created — a Brand page at /brands and the founder's page at /people. From there the brand is shown and promoted in many places across Ghar.tv — and every story, video and report links back to those two pages. Here's how it works, step by step.

Worked example Elleys — Electrical Switches & Accessories Category: Building Materials › Electricals & switches

Step by step

1

Join

The brand signs up with Ghar.tv

A business comes on board and picks a plan (Brand Connect). The plan decides how widely the brand is shown — Presence is the basic always-on page; Spotlight and Partner add stories, videos, events and more promotion.

Pick a plan

  • Presence — your page + listing + ads
  • Spotlight — adds stories, a homepage spot, an article in your name, enquiries
  • Partner — adds a brand video, podcast, events, reports & a promotion campaign

Show everywhere

The pages show up all across Ghar.tv

Depending on the plan, the brand & founder start appearing in many places on Ghar.tv. Each place creates something — a story, a video, a podcast, a report — and each one links back to /brands/elleys and /people/founder.

4

Promote & grow

Everything links back & keeps working for you

Every place links back to the brand & founder pages, so people always land on your Ghar.tv page. The pages get stronger with every appearance — more stories, videos and mentions collect on the same pages, and search & related links keep bringing people back.

How it keeps growing

  • Links back: every story, video, podcast & report points to /brands/elleys + /people/founder
  • Builds up: all of it collects on the two pages over time
  • Gets found: search & category pages keep showing them again
  • Spreads: creators & paid ads bring new people back to the pages

How it all connects

Four things, all linked together. This is what lets one brand show up everywhere — one page, linked from many places.

Brand

Company page · /brands/<name>

Person

Founder / team page · /people/<name>

Category

Industry, role, topic

Content

Story · video · podcast · report · ad

Brand ↔ Person  — a brand has founders / team; a person belongs to a brand
Brand & Person → Category  — so they show on the right category pages
Content → Brand + Person  — every piece links back to the pages it's about or by
