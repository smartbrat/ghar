The practice

Global names, Indian hands.

A brand-of-brands operating model: internationally recognised designers create the collections, master artisans in India make them, one Kolkata studio ties it all together.

Scarlet Splendour was founded in 2014 to answer a simple frustration: India was full of world-class craftsmanship but had no house presenting it to the world at a luxury level. Siblings Ashish and Suman Bajoria set out to change that.

The catalogue today runs to over 40 collections. Pieces by Matteo Cibic, Karim Rashid, Nika Zupanc, Richard Hutten, Marcantonio and Elena Salmistraro are each hand-finished in metal, glass, resin, marble and lacquered wood in the brand’s workshops. The pieces have shown at Salone del Mobile, been placed in celebrity homes across Mumbai and Milan, and been picked up by design press from AD to Wallpaper*.

The through-line, from the earliest Vanilla Noir cabinet to the most recent Land of Legends pieces, is drama. Scarlet doesn’t do quiet luxury. It does furniture that walks into a room and takes it over.
