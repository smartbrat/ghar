1942 Founded

Asian Paints has been colouring India’s walls since 1942 — starting with a single garage in Mumbai and growing into Asia’s third-largest paints company by revenue.

Today Asian Paints is present in more than 65,000 retailers and colours over 20 million homes in India every year. Beyond paint, the company runs Beautiful Homes (interior design service), Nilaya (wallpapers), Ador (adhesives), Sleek (modular kitchens & wardrobes), and White Teak (decorative lighting).

The Color of the Year programme has become a fixture of the Indian design calendar, and the annual Colour Next report is followed by architects, interior designers and paint contractors as the industry’s colour authority.

Amit Syngle MD & CEO, Asian Paints
