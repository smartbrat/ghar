Avirahi Group

Three decades of Mumbai residential craft, and Avirahi City, a 170-acre township rising at India’s first greenfield smart city, Dholera SIR.

1994 Established

Avirahi Group has spent three decades building homes and commercial spaces across Mumbai’s western suburbs, and is now channelling that craft into a 170-acre master-planned township at India’s first greenfield smart city.

Founded in 1994 as Abhigna Group, Avirahi has delivered residential apartments in Borivali, Malad, Dahisar and Goregaon, and commercial spaces at Avirahi Arcade and Avvirahi Platinum, the neighbourhood-scale work that made the group’s name.

Its flagship, Avirahi City, is a 1,200-plot integrated township at Dholera SIR, Gujarat, master-planned by Halcrow (UK) with architecture by The Grid, recognised by Franchise India as Ahmedabad Region’s Project of the Year.
