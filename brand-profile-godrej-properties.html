Godrej Properties is the real-estate arm of the 125-year-old Godrej Group. India’s largest listed developer by sales bookings, with residential, township and commercial projects across the country’s eight largest metros.

The property arm was spun out in 1990 and listed publicly on the BSE and NSE in 2010 — one of the first professionally-managed developers to be traded on Indian exchanges. Since then it has scaled from a Mumbai-focused practice to a nationwide book of residential, plotted, township and commercial development.

Recent recognition includes ranking among the top listed developers in India by sales bookings and delivered saleable area, along with the group’s wider commitments on sustainability, professionalisation and philanthropy that give the family name its enduring trust equity.
