2006 Founded

At Horizon Architects, architecture is more than building spaces. It is about shaping experiences that endure. Founded in 2006 by Ar. Hemal Shah, the firm has evolved into a multidisciplinary design practice delivering thoughtful, innovative and functional environments across India.

The strength of the practice lies in a seamlessly integrated approach. With dedicated teams across architecture, interiors, execution and management, Horizon ensures precision at every stage, from concept to completion.

With over 500 delivered projects spanning residential, commercial and interior spaces, Horizon Architects has built a reputation for handling complex developments with clarity and creativity. Since 2006, the firm has been shaping Mumbai and beyond with architecture that blends innovation, efficiency and aesthetics, guided by the founding conviction that great design is meticulous design.

Ar. Hemal Shah Founder, Horizon Architects
Services

  • Master Planning & Urban Design

    Townships, tech parks, resorts and campuses across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region and beyond, shaped for scale and long-term use.

  • Architecture

    Residential, commercial, institutional and mixed-use, with a design language that is forward-thinking and deeply functional.

  • Working Drawings & Detailed Design

    Site-ready drawings fully coordinated across RCC, MEP and liaisoning, with stage-wise issuance and active RFI support.

  • Facade Design & Execution

    Climate-responsive elevations with integrated facade lighting and end-to-end coordination with cladding, lighting and vendor partners.

  • Interior Design

    Residential and commercial interiors that carry the same rigour as the architecture, with material palettes tuned to purpose and everyday use.

  • 3D Visualisation & Presentations

    Photo-realistic renders, interactive walkthroughs, rapid facade iterations, and brochure-ready visuals for web, print and pitch.

  • Vastu-Integrated Planning

    Energy-balanced layouts and plot orientation in collaboration with Ar. Madhura Modi (Vaastu Vision), without compromising design intent.

  • Execution & Project Management

    Site delivery, coordination and PMC from concept through handover, backed by 70+ professionals and dedicated in-house teams.
