About

Saint-Gobain traces its roots to 1665, when it was chartered by Louis XIV to produce the mirrors of the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles. Today it is one of the world’s largest building-materials companies.

In India, Saint-Gobain has been present since 1996, with over 35 manufacturing plants across the country. The Indian arm produces flat glass, gypsum boards, insulation, industrial ceramics, mortars and abrasives — the material fabric of Indian construction, from single-storey homes to metro stations to airport terminals.

The brand portfolio includes Sekurit (auto glass), Glassolutions (architectural glass), Gyproc (drywall & ceilings), Weber (tile adhesives & mortars), ISOVER (insulation), Norton (abrasives) and CertainTeed (roofing). Saint-Gobain India has committed to net-zero carbon by 2050 across its manufacturing footprint.

    Work

    Automotive glazing · Since 1929

    Sekurit Auto Glass

    OEM glass for every major Indian automotive manufacturer — laminated windshields, tempered side and rear glass, heated glass and acoustic laminated units.
    Architectural glass · Full-service

    Glassolutions Facades

    End-to-end architectural glass solutions — from double-glazed units for office towers to structural glass for airports. Recent projects include several Bengaluru IT campuses and the T2 terminal at Bengaluru airport.
    Drywall system · Interior partitioning

    Gyproc Habito Drywall

    High-strength gypsum board with 3× the impact resistance of standard drywall. Used in schools, hospitals and high-traffic commercial spaces.
    Mortars & adhesives · Since 1902

    Weber Tile Adhesive

    India’s most-specified tile adhesive by architects — polymer-modified for large-format tiles, waterproofing systems, and industrial flooring.
    Thermal & acoustic insulation

    ISOVER Insulation

    Glass wool and stone wool insulation for buildings, industrial pipes, HVAC ducting and cold-storage. Certified by IGBC for green-building credit.
    Smart facade glass · Premium

    SageGlass Dynamic Glass

    Electronically tintable glass that adjusts opacity based on sunlight — used in premium office facades to reduce HVAC load and eliminate blinds.
    Residential roofing · Since 1904

    CertainTeed Roofing

    Asphalt-shingle and metal roofing systems — the go-to residential roofing brand in North America, expanding across select Indian projects.
    Industrial abrasives · Since 1885

    Norton Abrasives

    Grinding wheels, cutting discs and surface-conditioning products for every stage of construction and metal fabrication.
