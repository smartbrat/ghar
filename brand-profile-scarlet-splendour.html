About

Scarlet Splendour was founded in 2014 by siblings Ashish and Suman Kanodia to present Indian craftsmanship at a luxury level to the world.

The catalogue today runs to over 40 collections — pieces by Matteo Cibic, Karim Rashid, Nika Zupanc, Richard Hutten, Marcantonio and Elena Salmistraro are each hand-finished in metal, glass, resin, marble and lacquered wood in the brand’s workshops.

The pieces have shown at Salone del Mobile, been placed in celebrity homes across Mumbai and Milan, and been picked up by design press from AD to Wallpaper*.
