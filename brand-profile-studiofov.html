You dream it and we design it.

Founded by Manpreet Singh, Studio FOV is a boutique architectural visualisation studio equipped with experts from architecture and art, working from a head office in Delhi and a studio in Bandra West, Mumbai.

The studio makes CGI illustrations, 3D cinematic walkthroughs, 3D animation, virtual reality (360° panoramas and full real-time walkthroughs), physical scale models and interior design visualisation, for architects, interior designers, developers and government projects.

Its stated mission is to deliver high-quality physical models and architectural visualisations that help clients present, market and communicate their projects with clarity and confidence, on small- and large-scale projects alike, including townships, commercial hubs, industrial parks and mixed-use developments.

MS Manpreet Singh Founder
Services
  • 01

    3D renders

    Photoreal architectural stills for brochures, sales collateral, approval submissions and investor decks.

  • 02

    3D walkthroughs

    Cinematic navigations of an unbuilt building — from lobby to unit to view — for sales lounges and launch films.

  • 03

    3D animation

    Motion beyond the walkthrough: construction sequences, contextual weather, day-to-night cycles, product features.

  • 04

    Virtual & augmented reality

    Room-scale VR previews and AR overlays so a buyer or approver can walk the building before ground breaks.

  • 05

    Physical scale models

    Craft-scale models with realistic material and detail finishing — built in-house for exhibitions, sales offices and client presentations.

  • 06

    Interior design visualisation

    Full-context interior renders for architects and interior designers presenting to buyers or clients.
