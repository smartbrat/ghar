At the intersection of design and statute - four decades of Mumbai's redevelopment.

TEEARCH is a Borivali-based multidisciplinary consultancy delivering end-to-end architectural, liaisoning, project management and transaction advisory solutions for Mumbai's most complex redevelopment mandates.

Founded in 1982 by Tarun Motta and now led by a second-generation Motta partnership, the firm sits at the intersection of design and statute. It has helped shape Mumbai's Transfer of Development Rights policy and the DCPR 2034 regulations, the same rulebook it now navigates for its clients every day.

The practice runs cluster redevelopments, cooperative-society mandates, government schemes and institutional projects across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region: from single-plot societies to the 226-lakh-sq-ft BDD Chawl redevelopment for over 15,000 members.
