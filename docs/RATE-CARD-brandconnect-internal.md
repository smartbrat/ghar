# Brand Connect — Internal Rate Card (NOT FOR PUBLIC / CUSTOMER COPY)

> **Internal only.** Per the Brand Connect narrative-discipline rule, packages, à-la-carte
> framing and prices must **never** appear in public-facing copy (site pages, the media kit,
> decks shared before inquiry). The marketing team scopes the right engagement **1:1 after a
> brand connects** — Mode A (full 3-layer bundle) or Mode B (single services). This card is the
> reference the sales team uses in that conversation. All prices are **introductory early-partner
> rates, exclusive of GST.**

_Last updated 2026-08-07. Prices are a working starting point — confirm with leadership before quoting._

## Package names (2026-08-07 rename)

The packages were renamed from **Presence / Spotlight / Partner** to
**Listed / Featured / Signature** so the customer-facing badge on the card
uses the same word as the package they bought. No translation layer between
what sales quotes, what the customer signs for, and what the visitor sees.

Historical decks and emails will still reference the old names for a while.
Sales can say "Spotlight is now Featured, same deliverables" — the promise
didn't change, only the label. Rate is unchanged.

## Indicative engagement tiers (Mode A — bundled)

**Tiers are cumulative.** Every tier carries everything the tier below it
carries, plus its own additions — Signature is a **superset** of Featured,
which is a superset of Listed. When quoting Signature to a brand, describe
it as "everything in Listed + everything in Featured + these Signature-only
additions" — never as a standalone list. The "Featured +" and "Signature +"
prefixes below encode this literally.

| Tier | Indicative | Roughly includes |
|------|-----------|------------------|
| Listed | ₹75,000 / yr | Brand page (microsite), founder page, category listing, brand directory, newsletter mention, enquiry contact |
| Featured | ₹2,50,000 / yr | Listed + top-of-category, 2 sponsored stories, 2 features, In Focus placement on the category page (`/brands` or `/people`), placement on the relevant `/design/*` pillar page(s) the brand belongs in, banner ads, 1 expert article, reports |
| Signature | ₹7,50,000 / yr | Featured + 1 brand film (VideoWorks), 1 founder conversation (GharTalks), GharEvents presence, sponsored report, creator campaign, always-on ads across `/voices`, `/design/*` and category pages, account manager |

Tiers are priced **below** the sum of the à-la-carte items so bundling is the incentive.

## Placement on /brands and /people (what each tier LOOKS LIKE on the page)

The three tiers correspond to three placement states in the directory. This is
the visible deliverable — sales can point at it during a demo. See
[BRANDCONNECT-spotlight-delivery.md](./BRANDCONNECT-spotlight-delivery.md) for
the rotation math and the implementation.

| Tier | Grid placement | Card badge | Featured surface (post-MVP) |
|------|---------------|------------|-----------------------------|
| Listed | Alphabetical within category | None | None |
| Featured | Pinned to head of category (position 0) | `Featured` (warm Turmeric two-tone) | Rotates into the In Focus block. Share of days = share of package. |
| Signature | Pinned to head of category (position 0) | `Signature` (dark ink pill + Terracotta accent) | Permanent In Focus resident. Never rotates out. |

**Visual hierarchy is deliberate:** the Signature badge sits on a **darker**
pill than Featured, not just a different warm tint. A visitor scanning the
directory reads the tier without needing to remember which colour ranks
higher. Both share the icon-box treatment so they read as one family.

**MVP state (2026-08-07):** the In Focus block is hidden while the visual is
reworked. Every Featured and Signature tenant is still pinned to the head of
their category with their tier badge visible on the card. The "featured
surface" column above returns when the redesigned block ships. Sales language
does not change in the meantime — the pin + the badge honour the top-of-
category promise on their own.

**Why Listed carries no badge:** three tiers with three badges reads like
three grades of "featured" and devalues the top two. Listed is the entry
tier — the honest signal is that the card is here, in the directory, at all.
The badge is what Featured and Signature tenants pay to earn.

**Co-founder inheritance on /people:** every named founder of a paid brand
carries the brand's badge on their profile card, capped by role classification
(founders/named partners) not by count. A firm with 3 co-founders and 12
associates gets 3 badged cards, not 12. Detailed rule in
[BRANDCONNECT-spotlight-delivery.md §4](./BRANDCONNECT-spotlight-delivery.md).
